    How to use it

    1. Type your tag, or paste one in step 2.
    2. Highlight letters and pick a style, color, or gradient. Only the highlighted letters change.
    3. Copy the code above.
    4. Join your server, type /teamtag, paste the code, and press Enter.
    5. A staff member reviews it. Once approved, your tag shows in chat, the tab list, and above your head.

    Only team owners and managers can request a tag.
